Our point of view

A café for
curious people.

Darbar started at UPES with a simple belief: technology should make everyday life feel more human, not less. So we built a coffee bar that listens, learns, and still respects the slow parts.

Our beans are roasted in small lots, our food is made in our kitchen, and every recommendation leaves room for surprise.
