Privacy,
plain spoken.

Last updated: 18 March 2025

We keep it simple

When you order, reserve a table, or join our dispatch, we collect only what helps us serve you: your name, contact details, order preferences, and the details needed to complete your request.

Your data is yours

We do not sell personal information. We use it to process orders, confirm reservations, improve the Darbar experience, and send updates only when you ask for them. You can unsubscribe from dispatch emails at any time.

Questions?

Write to hello@coffeedarbar.in and a real person will reply.
