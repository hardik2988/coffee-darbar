Using Darbar

Coffee Darbar is a student-built café concept and digital product from UPES. By using this site, you agree to use it responsibly and provide accurate details for orders, reservations, and messages.

Orders and reservations

Menu prices, availability, and preparation times can change. We’ll always do our best to communicate changes. Reservations are held for 15 minutes, and online order simulations are for this capstone experience.

Our promise

We aim to make every interaction useful, accessible, and warm. If something doesn’t feel right, contact us at hello@coffeedarbar.in.
